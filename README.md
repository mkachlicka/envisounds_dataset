## Licences for audio files
The EnviSounds datset includes fragments of sounds downloaded from external repositories and respects the licensing under which original files were shared.

Please see below for the list of sources and relevant licence agreements.

### Cornell
The Cornell Lab of Ornithology eBird and Macaulay Library repositories contain files under the Cornell Media Licensing Agreement which can be read in full [here](https://support.ebird.org/en/support/solutions/articles/48000952192-ebird-media-licensing-agreement?__hstc=264660688.778e57a0b3e716ece6d878c827281757.1761320930602.1761320930602.1761320930602.1&__hssc=264660688.7.1761320930602&__hsfp=545870477&_ga=2.222316827.1021475277.1761321064-1364001953.1761321064). 

According to this license, the Cornell Lab retains the right to sublicense their library contents for research use, but does not allow the use of the content to any third party for commercial use without the contributor’s permission.

### BBC
The BBC’s Sound Effects library contains files under the BBC’s Content Licence for RemArc which can be read in full [here](https://sound-effects.bbcrewind.co.uk/licensing).

Under this license, BBC grants permission to use the BBC content but only for non-commercial, personal, formal educational or research purposes.

### Zapsplat
The audio files downloaded from the Zapsplat website were shared under the Zapsplat standard licence that can be found [here](https://www.zapsplat.com/license-type/standard-license/).

ZapSplat grants a worldwide, perpetual, non-exclusive license to use their sound effects and music tracks in your own projects.

### SoundBible
The audio files downloaded from SoundBible are royalty free, but whether they can be used for commercial purposes depends on whether a given recording was shared under a Creative Commons Attribution or Public Domain licence, and needs to be specified per each file. More information can be found on the [SoundBible about page](https://soundbible.com/about.php).
